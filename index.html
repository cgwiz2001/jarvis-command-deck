How this works

You only talk to Jarvis. With an xAI key, Jarvis answers instantly (spoken in Talk mode) and, when a job belongs to a specialist, adds it to Jarvis's inbox in Second Brain for that bot. Without a key, messages are simply added to the inbox. Second Brain then routes the job to the right bot automatically. This deck checks for updates every forty-five seconds and fires a beam when a request is routed or answered.
